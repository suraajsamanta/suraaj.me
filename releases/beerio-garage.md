# Beerio garage refresh

Thirteen brand-inspired, badge-free original vehicle meshes replace generic rides. Each has individual wheelbase, silhouette, light and rear details. Astute distinctions include the F40 wing and round rear lamps, the Chiron C-line and wide rear light, G63 rear spare, Panigale/BMW bike layout, and Cybertruck's stainless wedge profile. The displayed models are original low-poly game geometry, not engineering CAD.

Phone join and solo setup allow choosing a ride and paint. Swipe or drag the showroom, use the arrow keys, or tap Front, Left, Rear, or Right. The selected race mesh and paint use one on-demand WebGL canvas; four offline preview angles serve non-WebGL browsers. Character selection uses the real 3D-head portraits and a wider-screen spotlight. Legacy rides remain compatible; racing handling is unchanged.

Regenerate imagery with `node scripts/generate-character-models.mjs --garage`. The additive relay allowlist must be live before releasing the UI; it is already present on the 9a7a33f base.
